Analytical research contract: at most three independently mapped public issuers; target next-quarter reported inventory growth with consistent currency, units and accounting basis.
Baseline A: seasonal persistence plus lagged, previously published issuer financials.
Candidate B: exactly the baseline plus legally permitted importer-shipment activity available by each historical cutoff.

Those are dated access observations, not a fresh account entitlement check or an approved dataset. Current rights and provider availability must be verified before use. - exact provider/source/native entity IDs; issuer CIK and independent mapping evidence;
- event/measurement period, first publication, revision publication and retrieval times;
